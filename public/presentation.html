360° Single-Clip Package Scanner

CORE TECHNICAL INNOVATION

Seamless User Workflow

The officer opens the camera and rotates the product once in 8–10 seconds. Live on-screen rotation HUD reticle guides the turning speed without manual multi-angle tapping.

Laplacian Variance Blur Filtering

Samples 24–36 frames across the rotation. Discards motion-blurred frames using 2D discrete Laplacian convolution, retaining only high-frequency, crystal-clear label text.

360° Scan Coverage Matrix

Clusters frames into cardinal surfaces: Front, Right, Back, Left, Top, and Bottom. Shows 5/6 surfaces verified.

Explainable 3-State Verdict & 100-Point Scoring

CORE STATUTORY LOGIC
✓

COMPLIANT (85–100%)

All mandatory declarations are clearly verified, correctly formatted with statutory units (kg, g, L, ml), inclusive of all taxes, with valid manufacturer details.

⚠

NEEDS REVIEW (55–84%)

Essential declarations present, but secondary surfaces are partially captured or lighting is low. Routed to officer queue for visual sign-off without false penalties.

✕

NON-COMPLIANT (< 50%)

Conclusive evidence of statutory violations: missing MRP, deceptive packaging, unformatted net quantity, missing manufacturer identity, or expired commodity.

100-Point Weighted Scoring Index: MRP (15) + Net Qty (15) + Mfg Name (15) + Address (15) + Dates (15) + Name (10) + Consumer Care (10) + Origin (5)
